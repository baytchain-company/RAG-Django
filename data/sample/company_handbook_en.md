# Nova Labs — Employee Handbook (fictional example)

## Working hours
Our core hours are 10:00–16:00 Tashkent time. Outside core hours you can plan your day freely.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week. Fridays are meeting-free.

## Vacation and sick leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 paid vacation days per year. Unused days (up to 5) move to the next year.
Request vacation at least 2 weeks in advance in the HR portal.
Sick leave is paid from the first day; send a doctor's note to HR within 3 days.

## Equipment
New employees receive a MacBook Pro and a 27-inch monitor on day one.
A home-office budget of $300 is available once per year for chairs, desks or headphones.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a $1,000 yearly learning budget for courses, books and conferences.
Unused learning budget does not roll over.

## Salary and payments
Salaries are paid on the 5th of every month. If the 5th is a weekend, payment is made on the previous working day.
